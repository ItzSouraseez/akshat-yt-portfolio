THE WAY I
ANALYZE RETENTION

Not “the graph went down.”
What happened here — and what should change next?

Retention is where strategy becomes observable. I look for the moments where the promise, story or information density stops matching what the viewer expected.

Then I turn those observations into changes for the next video — not just a post-mortem.
